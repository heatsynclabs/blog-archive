- Zach Burnham, 16, shows of his award winning [haptic-feedback robotic hand](http://www.instructables.com/id/How-to-Build-a-Robotic-Hand-with-Haptic-Feedback). Photo by [Nate Caine](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/6906603734/).

- Photos from Mesa Second Friday Steampunk Street. Photo by [Moheeb Zara](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7179760212/).

- Nate Plamondon flies his RC helicopter with the aluminum rotor head he fabricated. Photo by [Ryan Rix](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7225277498/).

- Ryan Rix's lasercut parametric rocketry stands. Photo by [Ryan Rix](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7225303510/).

- Tanner and Aidan practiced their welding skills by making a claw weapon. Photo by [Nate Plamondon](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7225544978/).
