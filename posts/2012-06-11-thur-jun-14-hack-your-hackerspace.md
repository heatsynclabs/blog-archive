If you didn't know, we wanted to remind you this thursday, and every second and fourth thursday, is a [Hack Your Hackerspace](http://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/HYH). Your lab needs YOU to stop by, vote on new members and changes to the lab, and pick up a broom and clean up! HeatSync labs has no staff, just you! Even while our peers in town die off, we charge asbsurdly low membership rates and we never hard sell. This week, and every Hack Your Hackerspace is your time to give back and make HeatSync Labs work the rest of the month.

All HeatSync Labs meetups are open to the public for free; Cover charges are for Scottsdale night clubs! If you really want to give us money though, t-shirts, membership and sponsorship subscriptions will be available and hustled aggressively by our volunteers.

HeatSync Labs is located east of Robson on Main Street at:
