**Music Hacknight (Fri Jun 29) 7:00 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

Music hacknight has moved to its new home, fourth friday night. If you didn't know the ham shack/Den of the Disembodied Deer has become a music room complete with drums, synths, guitars, pedals amps and tons more. This month: open jam night. Bring any and all of your musical instruments tonight and lets see how much sound we can make.

All HeatSync Labs meetups are open to the public for free; Cover charges are for Scottsdale night clubs! If you really want to give us money though, t-shirts, membership and sponsorship subscriptions will be available and hustled aggressively by our volunteers.
