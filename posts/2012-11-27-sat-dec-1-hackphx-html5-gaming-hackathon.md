**HackPHX Html5 Gaming (Sat Dec 1) 10:00 am|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

This Saturday marks the first hackathon of the [hackPHX](http://hackphx.com/) season, HTML5 gaming!

The day will start off with [IcedDev](http://iceddev.com/) demonstrating the basics of HTML5 game development, continue with 12 hours of hacking (food provided), and end with game demos, the crowning of our champions, and 3 people going home with Nexus 7 tablets!

The event is completely free and open to any skill level but space is limited so please do RSVP at [http://hackphxhtml5game.eventbrite.com/](http://hackphxhtml5game.eventbrite.com/).

Big thanks to our sponsors HeatSync Labs and [IcedDev](http://iceddev.com/) without whom we couldn't make this happen!
