[!\[\](../images/gallery/front-page-stories/ps-yxcuwvoh-170x170-75.jpg)](http://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/zombie-tech/id473337591)

[Zombie Tech](http://zombietech.tv/), who bill themselves as "A forum for engineers, scientists, and inventors to ponder on the technologies needed to survive the inevitable zombie apocalypse!" have taken a break from interviewing electronics luminaries like [Joe Grand](http://www.zombietech.tv/tag/joe-grand/), [Dave Jones](http://www.zombietech.tv/2011/10/20/zombie-tech-episode-010-dave-jones/) and [Chris Gammell](http://www.zombietech.tv/2011/10/13/zombie-tech-episode-009-chris-gammell/) to cover AZ and HeatSync Labs in specific! In the past they have interviewed [Joey Hudy](http://www.zombietech.tv/2011/12/15/zombie-tech-episode-018-joey-hudy/), and local hacker [John S](http://www.zombietech.tv/2011/09/01/zombie-tech-episode-003-johns_az/ ), two weeks ago they talked to HeatSync Labs co-founder [Rickoooooo](http://www.zombietech.tv/2012/05/10/zombie-tech-episode-037-%e2%80%93-rick-osgood/) and this week they [interviewed Jacob Rosenthal](http://www.zombietech.tv/2012/05/24/zombie-tech-episode-039-jacob-rosenthal/ ) on HeatSync Labs and hackerspaces, [his trip to China](http://citizengadget.com/post/23086291751/another-white-guy-spends-30-days-in-china-and-thinks-he) and resulting [interview with Mitch Altman](http://citizengadget.com/post/23174345733/interview-mitch-altmans-hacker-trip-to-china-2), and his goals in ubiquitous computing and body modification.

[Check it out!](http://www.zombietech.tv/2012/05/24/zombie-tech-episode-039-jacob-rosenthal/ )
