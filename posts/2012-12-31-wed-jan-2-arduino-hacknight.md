**Arduino Night  (Wed Jan 2) 07:00 pm|az @ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall**

If you don't know how these normally go down, electronics (Arduino, TI, Raspberry PI, Chipkit, we don't play favorites) hackers of all skill levels (including beginners) are invited to stop into Heatsync every first Wednesday of the month to hack shoulder to shoulder with their peers.

This week come brush up on your Arduino skills and meet other Arduino hackers in the valley who you'll definitely be seeing again in a month or so when you're hacking side by side with them for 12+ hours at our upcoming hardware hackathon!

Thats right, the secret ingredient is on its way, and that means we're about to announce the date. You've [signed up for early invite to hackphx hardware](http://hackphx.com/arduino_winter2012/), havent you? Sponsorship has been awesome and its looking to be a huge event, but seats are limited!

Bring your project and or just your passion and laptop (we've got the Arduinos and sensors covered) and come hack with us.
