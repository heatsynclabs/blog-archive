**Hot Topics (Thur Nov 15) 7:00 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

Hot Topics is our tragically named lightning talks series modeled on events like Ignite, Ted, and [Noisebridge's Five Minutes of Fame](http://www.noisebridge.net/wiki/Five_Minutes_of_Fame). Talks are <5 minutes and generally revolve around creation and hackerspace culture.

Thursday's talks will include:


- [Blaine Bublitz](https://twitter.com/BlaineBublitz)/[Luis Montes](https://twitter.com/monteslu) - [A look at Iced Dev's beginning, present and future](http://www.youtube.com/watch?feature=player_detailpage&v=lgdHI-dVAOQ). (Iced Dev is a software business that they run)

- [Mike Syfritt](https://twitter.com/AestheticMINIs)- [The process of molding and casting](http://www.youtube.com/watch?feature=player_detailpage&v=uVgjxqS6Org).

- [Mark Kirschenbaum](http://www.facebook.com/trunk420)- [How to bring an idea from concept to reality](http://www.youtube.com/watch?feature=player_detailpage&v=6OagxwOEeyk).  Mark will be speaking about his experience designing a physical product, and then bringing it to market.

- [Tim Gerrits](http://twitter.com/ancipes)- [Human Perception for Hackers](http://www.youtube.com/watch?feature=player_detailpage&v=SpwqgtK--3g) - Neurological foundations for user interface design.

- [Colin Ho](http://twitter.com/ohcolinho)- [Open Source Robotics](http://www.youtube.com/watch?feature=player_detailpage&v=O7gmbS2ssVY) - Colin will talk about his experience building robots, and how the Open Source philosophy is enabling both innovation, and accessibility, as well as bringing us closer to ubiquitous robotics.

- [Zach Julian](http://rootthebox.com/) - [Root The Box](http://www.youtube.com/watch?feature=player_detailpage&v=7UFpOVC6YpM) - A student lead computer hacking (CTF) game that requires skill, speed and team work. Join FREE January 18th-19th in Chandler Arizona!
