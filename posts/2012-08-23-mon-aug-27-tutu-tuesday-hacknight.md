** Knitting and Crocheting Hacknight (Mon Aug 27th) 6:30 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

On October 2nd the MAC is having an [event](http://www.mesaartscenter.com/index.php/events/free-events/tututuesday ) to raise money for breast cancer. On Monday 27th we will be hosting a event for volunteers to help out by making tutus to sell for the event.  There is no sewing required.  All materials and instructions will be provided.  Please come and help out for a good cause.
