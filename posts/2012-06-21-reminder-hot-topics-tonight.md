**Hot Topics (Thur Jun 21) 7:00 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

Hot Topics is our tragically named lightning talks series modeled on events like Ignite, Ted, and [Noisebridge's Five Minutes of Fame](http://www.noisebridge.net/wiki/Five_Minutes_of_Fame). Talks are <5 minutes and generally revolve around creation and hackerspace culture. Tonights talks will include:


- [Jacob Rosenthal](http://jacobrosenthal.com) - [Eagle -> Sketchup -> 3D print](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=72M6l-owuxg&feature=g-all-u)

- [Nathan Plamondon](http://blag.meznak.net) - [Modern Tools for Hobbyist Rocketry](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gBCt3zIFfDI&feature=g-all-u)

- [Tim Gerrits](http://timgerrits.com) - [5 Robots in 3 Weeks](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j8ZjG_ECU1Y&feature=plcp)

- [Ryan McDermott](http://thingist.com/users/Ryan) - [Making My Niece's World Bigger](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Kpq3_NS4ss&feature=em-uploademail)

- [Max and Mike](http://www.makermaximum.com/) - [Hydro Bot](http://www.makermaximum.com/2012/04/hydrobot-presentation.html)* postponed*

- And you?

Don't be scared, its totally informal and fun. Send an email to info at heatsynclabs .org with your topic and title and we'll look to program you for this month or next.
