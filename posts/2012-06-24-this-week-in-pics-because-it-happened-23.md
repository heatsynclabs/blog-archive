- [Ryan McDermott](http://twitter.com/#!/blhack) used a technique called 'kerf bend' or 'living hinge' to make a [prototype for a bracelet](http://www.instructables.com/id/Wooden-bracelet/) he'll be giving away at burning man this year. [Photo by Ryan McDermott](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7395573858/in/photostream).

- Brian Korsedal vector etched some designs in to plywood to turn in to tables. [Photo by Brian Korsedal](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7416388120/in/photostream/ ).

- [Ryan McDermott](http://twitter.com/#!/blhack) made these beautiful acrylic boxes with wood inlay for the new Raspberry Pi. [Photo by Ryan McDermott.](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7425230948/in/photostream/)

- Shannon McDermott created a design and Ryan McDermott helped her [laser cut it into a shirt](http://www.instructables.com/id/Laser-Cut-Shirt/). [Photo by Ryan McDermott](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7382249758/in/photostream).

- [Moheeb Zara](http://twitter.com/#!/virgilvox) created a light up TARDIS on the laser cutter. [Photo by Moheeb Zara](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7399563908/in/photostream).

- [Prescott Ogden](http://twitter.com/#!/prescottogden) begins work on a helmet from Skyrim using the Ultimaker. [Photo by Prescott Ogden](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7395592442/in/photostream).

- Chad Stearns used the new whiteboard tables to draw out a big diagram of his electronic synth project. [Photo by Chad Stearns](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7379584986/ ).

- [Jennifer Plamandon](http://twitter.com/#!/gadgetdoll) etched an image from Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword onto the back of a mirror.Photo by [Jennifer Plamandon](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hslphotosync/7412563786/in/photostream ).
