**Hot Topics (Thur Sept 20) 7:00 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

Hot Topics is our tragically named lightning talks series modeled on events like Ignite, Ted, and [Noisebridge's Five Minutes of Fame](http://www.noisebridge.net/wiki/Five_Minutes_of_Fame). Talks are <5 minutes and generally revolve around creation and hackerspace culture. Thursday's talks will include:


- [Will Bradley](http://zyphon.com/): [Logic Analyzers and Arduino](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TkSGT-T9uXo&list=UUqResvAb8AJGooyoB7TZzow&index=2&feature=plcp)

- [Chad Stearns](http://chadcs.blogspot.com/): [A brief overview of the entire field of ethics](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pb67ptRoHl0&list=UUqResvAb8AJGooyoB7TZzow&index=1&feature=plcp)

- [Maker Maximum](http://www.makermaximum.com/): [Hydrobot 2.0](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpsMOvviZRU&list=UUqResvAb8AJGooyoB7TZzow&index=6&feature=plcp)

- [Ryan McDermott](http://thingist.com/users/Ryan) - [Hackerspaces as 21st Century Libraries](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dY7gjuJMgXU&list=UUqResvAb8AJGooyoB7TZzow&index=7&feature=plcp)

- [Ingrid Avendaño](http://ingridavendano.com/): [Hack Phoenix](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz3bofUqpc8&list=UUqResvAb8AJGooyoB7TZzow&index=5&feature=plcp)

- [Jacob Rosenthal](http://www.citizengadget.com): [Ubiquitous Computing via Body Modification](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RNx3AtNQ7Dg&list=UUqResvAb8AJGooyoB7TZzow&index=4&feature=plcp)

- [Luis Montes](https://plus.google.com/103067286453763274834/photos): [The magic of XKCD #1110](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hLD45f0KxYc&list=UUqResvAb8AJGooyoB7TZzow&index=3&feature=plcp)
