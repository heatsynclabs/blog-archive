# This Week: (Jun 18) Lathe Classes, (Jun 20) 3D Printing, (Jun 21) Hot Topics, (Jun 23) ARRL Field Day

*We have got your weeknights covered. Photo by Jacob Rosenthal*


**Lathe classes (Mon Jun 18) 6:30 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Machine Shop
Hurry! 1 ticket left. Register on our [guestlist](http://guestlistapp.com/events/108615). Participants will learn the basics of turning metal as well as safety and the specifics of the HSL mini lathe. Operations will include turning parting, facing, boring and drilling on both aluminum and steel.

**3D Printing (Wed Jun 20) 7:00 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall
Bring your Makerbot, Rep Rap, etc or just your curiosity. Print stuff, hack on machines, and talk about the 3d printing and prototyping industry. Check out our new Ultimaker and Austin West's new [Mendel 90](http://reprap.org/wiki/Mendel90) build!

**Hot Topics (Thur Jun 21) 7:00 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall
Our tragically named lightning talks series returns to Thursdays this month. Talks confirmed so far include robots, printing your eagle boards, and building a step sequencer!

**Mesa ARC Ham Radio Field Day (Sat Jun 23) 9:00 am|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall
This Saturday HeatSync Labs is your home base for the [ARRL amateur radio field day](http://www.arrl.org/field-day). Join Mesa Amateur Radio Club(WM7RC) starting at 9:00am and throughout the day as they take over the Kipp Hall with ham radio chatter.

All HeatSync Labs meetings are open to the public for **free**;  Cover charges are for Scottsdale night clubs!  If you really want to give us money though, t-shirts, membership and sponsorship subscriptions will be available and hustled aggressively by our volunteers.

HeatSync Labs is located east of Robson on Main Street at:
