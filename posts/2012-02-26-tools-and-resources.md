Summary of tools:

Wood/plastic:


- 100 watt laser cutter (CO2, 4x3 bed, DXF or B&W BMP format)

- Ultimaker & Makerbot "Cupcake" 3D printer

- Drill press

- Hand and power tools

- Radial arm saw (wood-cutting)

- Scroll saw (wood-cutting)

Metal:


- Mini-mill, mini-lathe, full lathe

- Full mill on loan

- Metal-cutting bandsaw

- Bench grinders

- MIG, TIG Welder

Electronics, communication, telecom & security:


- Arduino and accessories

- Soldering, hot air, desoldering station, and PCB prototyping

- Electronics testing bench with oscilloscope

- Laptops, penetration/security testing servers, wifi, Internet access

- HAM radio station
