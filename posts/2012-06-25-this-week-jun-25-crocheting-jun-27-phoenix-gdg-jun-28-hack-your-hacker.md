**Geeky Knitting and Crocheting (Mon Jun 25) 7:30 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall
Jen Plamondon is hosting this week's Fiber Arts meetup, teaching attendees how to crochet Jayne Cobb's Hat or Hogwarts house scarf.

**Google Developer Group (Wed Jun 27) 7:00 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall
A big welcome to the Phoenix GDG who are going to start meeting @heatsynclabs on fourth Wednesdays! This week a Google IO viewing party all day and a meetup afterwards.

**Hack Your Hackerspace(Thur Jun 28) 7:00 pm|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall
Uncle HeatSync needs you! We'd like to purchase new things for the lab like a band saw and tshirts and polos to sell, pass operating budgets, and more. If you're a member get out here and vote, and even if you're not tonight is the night to give your love and attention to make HeatSync awesome for the rest of the month.

**Music Hacknight(Fri Jun 29) 7:00 am|az **@ HeatSync Labs - Chamber of the Disembodied Deer
Chad Stearns continues his Music Hacknight series on a new day, fourth Fridays. Come down and hack on some instruments and make noise in our burgeoning studio upstairs.

**The Strand Record Release(Sat Jun 30) 9:00 am|az **@ Chasers Nightclub
The Strand joined us in May to shoot their new record release video, and we're all going out to Chaser Nightclub to see the lab on the big screen. Door ar 8:30, we'll start filing in afer 9p and The Strand won't probably go on stage until closer to midnight.

All HeatSync Labs meetings are open to the public for **free**; Cover charges are for Scottsdale night clubs! If you really want to give us money though, t-shirts, membership and sponsorship subscriptions will be available and hustled aggressively by our volunteers.
