Cool stuff happens in the lab all the time. To help document proof of cool stuff happening in the lab, we now have a [borrowed] Nikon D5000 equipped with an EyeFi card you can use at your pleasure to automatically upload photos to our hslphotosync Flickr account! Photos taken will be attributed to HeatSync Labs to be manually curated, labeled, tagged, and licensed under a Creative Commons license.  The camera is located on the lab bookshelf near the Guardians of Hades weird singing dolls.  Try it out, if you want to.  If you don't like Nikon and want to use your own camera, join our Flickr group and contribute photos online!
