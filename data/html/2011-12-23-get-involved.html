Come to the lab:

Hackerspaces are PLACES. Come meet us! We're open to the public M-F 7pm-10pm (and often outside these hours) for FREE! Bring your projects and your passion -- we promise we won't bite. Or come out for something specific, check out our calendar on our events page. We've got wifi, air conditioning and tons of tools and hardware to make your project a reality.
