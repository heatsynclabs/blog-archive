Lathe classes (Mon Jun 18) 6:30 pm|az @ HeatSync Labs - Machine Shop
Hurry! 1 ticket left. Register on our guestlist. Participants will learn the basics of turning metal as well as safety and the specifics of the HSL mini lathe. Operations will include turning parting, facing, boring and drilling on both aluminum and steel.

Hot Topics (Thur Jun 21) 7:00 pm|az @ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall
Our tragically named lightning talks series returns to Thursdays this month. Talks confirmed so far include robots, printing your eagle boards, and building a step sequencer!

This Saturday HeatSync Labs is your home base for the ARRL amateur radio field day. Join Mesa Amateur Radio Club(WM7RC) starting at 9:00am and throughout the day as they take over the Kipp Hall with ham radio chatter.
