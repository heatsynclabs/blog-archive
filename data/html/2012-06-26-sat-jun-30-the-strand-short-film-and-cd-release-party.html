Local industrial band The Strand (@strandland) saw Will's HeatSync tour on Hack-a-day and asked on our forums if they could use our lab as a video shoot location for their upcoming cd Combat Advantage, which they successfully kickstarted back in March. Will offered to liaison and John Kitt, long time fan and supporter of the band, built the doomsday gun for the short film (and ended up making himself useful on the production crew all day!)

On Saturday May 12th they turned our metal shop into a mad science lab using the sciency stuff we've been obsessively collecting for the past 3 years. They also used the ham shack as, what else, a communications room.

We're headed to the release party at Chasers Nightclub this Saturday to see the result and hear the new album and we hope you'll join us!
