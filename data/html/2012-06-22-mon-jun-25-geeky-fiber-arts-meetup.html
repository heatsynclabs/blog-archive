Fiber Arts Meetup (Mon Jun 25) 7:30 pm|az @ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

Remember the knitting, sewing, and crochet set meet EVERY Monday! Jen Plamondon is hosting this week's Fiber Arts meetup, teaching attendees how to crochet Jayne Cobb's Hat or Hogwarts house scarf!

A few supplies will be available at cost in the lab if you're entirely new. Otherwise bring a needle/hook and the color yarn you desire.

For the hat :

  • 1 skeins each of:
  • -Red Heart Super Saver in Gold (198g, 4 wt.)
  • -Lion Brand Vanna's Choice in Rust (100g, 4 wt.)
  • -Red Heart Collage in Rust (100g, 4 wt.)
  • 5.00 mm (K) Crochet Hook

For the scarf:

  • Gryffindor - Scarlet/Burgandy and Gold
  • Hufflepuff - Yellow/Gold and Black
  • Ravenclaw - Blue and Bronze
  • Slytherin - Green and Silver
  • 7 Ounces of Main Color
  • 2 Ounces or more of contrasting color
  • Hook Size I (5.50mm)
