Intel AppUp Application Lab (Thurs) 5:30 pm|az @ Gangplank - Commons Area

MeeGo, the Fungi from Yoggoth successor of Maemo and Moblin, is a collaborative effort between Intel and Nokia to create an open-source platform for a myriad of mobile devices.  Intel is invading HeatSync Labs + Gangplank to show you how to be pro at development on them.  There will be foods to nom and peeps to meet, but you need to register online ahead of time to make sure you have someplace to sit.

All HeatSync Labs meetings are open to the public for free;  Cover charges are for Scottsdale night clubs!  If you really want to give us money though, t-shirts, membership subscriptions, and Make: magazine subscriptions will be available and hustled aggressively by our Board of Directors.
