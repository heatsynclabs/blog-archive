Tear Apart Night (Fri Nov 30) 7:00 pm|az @ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

A lot of technologies are sold as boxes that do our magic for us, but what actually makes these devices tick? Our recurring Tear Apart Nights help to demystify the inner workings of our technologies and get rid of the stigma behind voiding the warranty on things you own.

We'll be clearing out our storage area of excess unused equipment, electronics, components, and more by:

  • Having Red Dot labeled totes with piles you can rummage through to grab and keep for future projects. Come grab this stuff before we recycle it or tear it apart.
  • Dissecting the technology and learning about the internal workings.
  • Collecting useful components for future projects and/or to add to our tear apart parts wall for the community to use.
  • Recycle the waste that is left over from the night with Mesa's upcoming recycle events.

Regain ownership of your possessions! Help us upcycle or reuse what would normally go into the trash!
