Memberships

Associate Membership is $25 per month, and confers:

  • the right to have your name on the list of members,
  • access to the lab and shared equipment during the published hours of operation,
  • and discounts with local manufacturers and suppliers.
  • (other membership rights listed herein, such as board election voting rights, do not apply.)

Basic Membership is $50 per month, and confers the benefits of Associate Membership, plus:

  • personal project space for a banker box (first box provided by HeatSync Labs),
  • the ability to direct HeatSync Labs with your vote in board elections,
  • and IF the community votes to grant you the privilege, 24/7 access to the lab including a key to the building and security code.

Plus Membership is $100 per month, and you get all the benefits of a basic membership, except:

  • a locker in the lab instead of a bankers box.
For more about being a member, read about our rules and community voting system.
