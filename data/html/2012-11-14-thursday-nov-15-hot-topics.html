Hot Topics (Thur Nov 15) 7:00 pm|az @ HeatSync Labs - Kipp Hall

Hot Topics is our tragically named lightning talks series modeled on events like Ignite, Ted, and Noisebridge's Five Minutes of Fame. Talks are <5 minutes and generally revolve around creation and hackerspace culture.

Thursday's talks will include:

  • Blaine Bublitz/Luis Montes - A look at Iced Dev's beginning, present and future. (Iced Dev is a software business that they run)
  • Mike Syfritt- The process of molding and casting.
  • Mark Kirschenbaum- How to bring an idea from concept to reality.  Mark will be speaking about his experience designing a physical product, and then bringing it to market.
  • Tim Gerrits- Human Perception for Hackers - Neurological foundations for user interface design.
  • Colin Ho- Open Source Robotics - Colin will talk about his experience building robots, and how the Open Source philosophy is enabling both innovation, and accessibility, as well as bringing us closer to ubiquitous robotics.
  • Zach Julian - Root The Box - A student lead computer hacking (CTF) game that requires skill, speed and team work. Join FREE January 18th-19th in Chandler Arizona!
